VEHICLE DOCUMENT GUIDE

Vehicle Document Requirements: What Documents Do You Need?

Vehicle requirements vary depending on the country, vehicle type, ownership status and the procedure you need to complete. This guide explains common documents that may be required for vehicle registration, ownership transfers, licensing, insurance and related services.

Common Vehicle Documents

  • Valid identification document or passport
  • Vehicle registration certificate or ownership document
  • Proof of vehicle purchase or ownership
  • Vehicle inspection or roadworthiness certificate
  • Valid vehicle insurance certificate
  • Driving licence, where required
  • Import or customs documents for imported vehicles
  • Proof of address, where required
  • Payment receipts for registration, taxes or fees

Vehicle Registration

When registering a vehicle, authorities may require proof of ownership, identification, vehicle inspection documents, insurance and evidence that applicable taxes or fees have been paid.

Transfer of Vehicle Ownership

If a vehicle is sold or transferred to another person, the authorities may require documents from both the buyer and seller. These can include the vehicle ownership certificate, identification documents, a sales agreement and proof of applicable payments.

Imported Vehicles

Imported vehicles may require additional documents such as customs clearance records, import declarations, purchase invoices and inspection certificates. Requirements depend on the country and the vehicle's origin.

Driving Licence

A driving licence is separate from vehicle ownership. Applicants may need identification, photographs, medical or testing documents and proof of payment depending on the licensing authority.

Vehicle Insurance

Many countries require vehicles to have valid insurance before they can legally be driven on public roads. Insurance requirements and accepted documents vary by jurisdiction.

Important Note

Vehicle documentation requirements differ from one country and authority to another. Always confirm the latest requirements with the relevant transport, vehicle registration, licensing or customs authority.
