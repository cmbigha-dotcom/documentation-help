Birth Certificate Requirements: What Documents Do You Need?

Birth certificate requirements vary by country, civil registry authority, place of birth and the type of request. This guide explains documents and steps commonly involved when applying for, replacing or correcting a birth certificate.

Common Birth Certificate Requirements

Depending on the authority and type of application, you may be asked for some or all of the following:

  • A completed birth certificate application or request form.
  • Identification for the person making the request.
  • Details about the birth, such as the person's full name, date and place of birth.
  • Information or identification for parents, where required.
  • Existing birth records or a previous certificate, when available.
  • Proof of relationship or authorization when requesting another person's record.
  • Payment of the applicable official certificate or processing fee.

How to Apply for a Birth Certificate

  1. Identify the civil registry or government authority responsible for birth records.
  2. Check the official requirements for your location and type of request.
  3. Prepare identification and the available birth information.
  4. Complete the official application or online request, if available.
  5. Pay the applicable official fee.
  6. Submit the request and follow the authority's instructions for collection or delivery.

Replacing a Lost Birth Certificate

If a birth certificate has been lost or damaged, the responsible civil registry may allow you to request a replacement or certified copy. Identification and information that helps locate the original record may be required.

Correcting Information on a Birth Certificate

Corrections to names, dates, parent information or other details may require additional evidence. The correction procedure depends on the authority and the nature of the error.

Important: Requirements Are Country-Specific

Birth registration systems, fees, application methods and document requirements differ between countries and authorities. Before applying, always verify the current requirements with the official civil registry or government authority responsible for the record.
