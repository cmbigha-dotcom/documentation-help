FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does DocuHelp issue official documents?

No. DocuHelp provides information and guidance. Documents must be issued by the relevant official authority.

Can you help me know what documents I need?

Yes. The portal is designed to organize document requirements and help users understand what to prepare.

Are requirements the same in every country?

No. Requirements can vary by country, authority, nationality and purpose. Always verify the final requirements with the relevant authority.

Can I request personal assistance?

Yes. Use the request form above to describe what you need.
