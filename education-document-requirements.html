EDUCATION DOCUMENT GUIDE

Education Document Requirements: What Documents Do You Need?

Education document requirements vary by school, university, employer, country and purpose. This guide explains common academic documents requested for admission, employment, applications and other official procedures.

Common Education Documents

Depending on the institution or authority, you may be asked for some or all of the following:

  • Academic certificates or diplomas.
  • School or university transcripts.
  • Examination results or official result statements.
  • Proof of enrollment or student status.
  • Admission or acceptance letters.
  • Identification documents or passport.
  • Academic qualifications and professional certificates.
  • Certified copies or officially authenticated documents, when required.
  • Certified translations when documents are issued in another language.

Applying to a School or University

An educational institution may request academic records, examination results, identification, application forms, recommendation letters, proof of language ability or other supporting documents.

Transcripts and Academic Records

A transcript normally provides a record of courses, subjects, grades or results completed at an educational institution. Some institutions require transcripts to be sent directly by the school or university.

Certificates and Diplomas

Certificates and diplomas may be required to prove that a qualification or course has been completed. Some applications may require certified copies, verification or authentication of these documents.

Important: Requirements Are Institution-Specific

There is no single education document checklist that applies everywhere. Requirements can differ between schools, universities, employers, government authorities and countries.

Before submitting an application, always confirm the current document requirements with the relevant school, university, employer or official authority.
