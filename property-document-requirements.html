Property Document Requirements: What Documents Do You Need?

Property documentation requirements vary depending on the country, type of property, ownership status and the transaction or procedure being completed. This guide explains common documents that may be required when buying, selling, transferring, registering or managing property.

Common Property Documents

  • Valid identification document or passport
  • Proof of property ownership
  • Land title or title deed
  • Property sale or purchase agreement
  • Property survey or site plan, where required
  • Property tax or land tax receipts
  • Transfer or registration documents
  • Proof of address, where required
  • Payment receipts for applicable fees and taxes

Buying Property

When purchasing property, buyers may need identification, a purchase agreement, proof of payment and documents confirming the seller's ownership. Depending on the jurisdiction, additional searches, certificates or approvals may also be required.

Selling Property

A property seller may need to provide proof of ownership, identification, property records, tax documents and other certificates required by the relevant authority.

Transfer of Ownership

Transferring property ownership may require a completed transfer application, identification documents, the existing title or ownership certificate, a sale or transfer agreement and proof that applicable taxes and fees have been paid.

Property Registration

Property registration requirements differ between jurisdictions. Authorities may request title documents, survey plans, identification, tax records and evidence of ownership or lawful transfer.

Important Note

Property laws and documentation requirements vary significantly from one country and authority to another. Always confirm the latest requirements with the appropriate land, property, registration or government authority before completing a transaction.
