Passport Requirements: What Documents Do You Need?

Passport requirements vary by country, nationality, age, application type and the authority handling the application. This guide explains the documents and steps commonly requested when applying for or renewing a passport.

Common Passport Requirements

Depending on your country and personal situation, you may be asked for some or all of the following:

  • A completed passport application or online pre-application.
  • Proof of identity, such as an accepted national identity document.
  • Proof of citizenship or nationality, such as a birth or citizenship document.
  • Recent passport photographs that meet the official specifications, where required.
  • Supporting documents for a name change or other personal-detail change, when applicable.
  • Your previous passport when applying for renewal or replacement, if available.
  • Payment of the applicable official passport fee.

How a Passport Application Usually Works

  1. Check the official passport authority's requirements for your country and application type.
  2. Prepare the required identity, citizenship and supporting documents.
  3. Complete the online or paper application according to the official procedure.
  4. Book an appointment or attend an enrolment centre if required.
  5. Provide photographs, fingerprints or other biometric information when required.
  6. Pay the official fee and keep your receipt or application reference.
  7. Follow the official instructions for passport collection or delivery.

Passport Renewal

Renewal requirements can differ from first-time passport applications. The authority may ask for your current or expired passport, updated photographs, identification and additional documents depending on your circumstances.

Lost, Stolen or Damaged Passport

If your passport is lost, stolen or damaged, follow the official authority's replacement procedure. You may need a police report, identity documents, proof of citizenship and additional supporting documents.

Passport Requirements for Children

Children may have additional requirements. Depending on the country, these can include a birth certificate, parental consent, identification documents for parents or guardians, photographs and the child's presence during the application or enrolment process.

Important: Requirements Are Country-Specific

There is no single worldwide passport checklist. Requirements, fees, appointment systems, processing times and accepted documents are set by each country's competent authority.

Before submitting an application, always verify the current requirements with the official passport authority, embassy or consulate responsible for your application.
