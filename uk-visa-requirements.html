UK Standard Visitor Visa Requirements: Documents and Application Steps

The United Kingdom's Standard Visitor route may be used for tourism, visiting family or friends, certain business activities and some short courses. The correct permission depends on your nationality and planned activities. Some travellers need a visitor visa, some need an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA), and others may not need either.

Important: This guide provides general information, not legal advice or a guarantee of eligibility. Always check the latest official UK government requirements for your nationality and circumstances.

1. Check Whether You Need a Visa

Before preparing documents, check whether you need a Standard Visitor visa, an ETA, or neither. Requirements depend on nationality, travel document, purpose of travel and current UK rules.

Check whether you need a UK visa — GOV.UK

2. What Is the Standard Visitor Route?

The Standard Visitor route can cover eligible tourism, family visits, certain business activities, some short courses and other permitted activities. Visitors generally cannot take employment with a UK company or work as self-employed, except for activities specifically allowed under the visitor rules.

Read the official Standard Visitor guidance — GOV.UK

3. Common Supporting Documents

The exact documents depend on your circumstances. Use this list to prepare for checking the official instructions; it is not a universal checklist.

  • A valid passport or travel document.
  • Information about the purpose and duration of the visit.
  • Planned travel dates and accommodation details, where applicable.
  • Evidence that you can pay for your trip and support yourself, or evidence of support from another person.
  • Relevant information about your work, studies, family responsibilities or personal circumstances.
  • If another person is paying, evidence of the support they will provide and their ability to provide it, where requested.
  • Any additional documents required for your particular circumstances or activities.

Documents not in English or Welsh may need a translation that meets official requirements. Check the current instructions before submitting documents.

Official application and document guidance — GOV.UK

4. How to Apply

  1. Check whether you need a visa or ETA.
  2. Confirm that the Standard Visitor route fits your purpose of travel.
  3. Read the official eligibility and document instructions.
  4. Complete the online application if a visitor visa is required.
  5. Pay the current official fee.
  6. Attend the required appointment and provide biometrics or documents if instructed.
  7. Wait for a decision and follow the official instructions.

GOV.UK says a Standard Visitor visa application can usually be made up to three months before travel. Confirm the current process and timing on the official website before applying.

Apply for a Standard Visitor visa — GOV.UK

5. Fees and Processing Times

Visa fees and processing times can change and may depend on the application and location. Check the official government website for the current fee and the relevant processing-time information. Avoid relying on outdated amounts or unofficial estimates.

Check UK visa processing times — GOV.UK

6. Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Assuming every nationality needs the same travel permission.
  • Using an old document checklist without checking current rules.
  • Submitting incomplete or inconsistent information.
  • Assuming that providing documents guarantees approval.
  • Relying on unofficial websites instead of the relevant government authority.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does everyone need a UK visitor visa?

No. Depending on nationality and circumstances, a traveller may need a visitor visa, an ETA, or neither. Use the official GOV.UK checker.

How early can I apply?

GOV.UK says applications for a Standard Visitor visa can usually be made up to three months before planned travel. Check the latest instructions before applying.

Does submitting all the documents guarantee approval?

No. Each application is assessed according to eligibility and the individual's circumstances.

Can I work in the UK as a visitor?

Standard Visitors generally cannot take employment with a UK company or work as self-employed, except for specifically permitted activities. Check the official rules for your exact activity.
